About This Service

Your electrical panel is the heart of your home's power system. Many older homes have 100A or 150A panels that struggle to support modern demands like EV chargers, heat pumps, and smart home systems. Our panel upgrade services bring your electrical infrastructure into the 21st century with increased capacity, modern safety features, and EV-ready circuits. Whether you're preparing for an EV charger, adding square footage, or simply upgrading an outdated panel, our licensed electricians deliver safe, code-compliant installations that pass inspection the first time.
