Solar questions, answered

Which Tesla solar panel do you install?

We install the Tesla Solar Panel TSP-420, a 420 watt monocrystalline panel assembled in Buffalo, New York.

Do I need a Powerwall with my solar panels?

No, but it is a good idea. A grid tied solar system without a battery generally shuts down during a power outage. Pairing the panels with a Powerwall 3 lets you keep essential circuits on.

Can I add a gas generator too?

Yes. Some customers pair solar and a Powerwall with a gas generator for extended outages. We can install the generator and transfer switch as part of the same project.

Will I need an electrical panel upgrade?

Sometimes. Older or full panels may need an upgrade or a sub-panel to take on solar. We check this during your free consultation. See our panel upgrade services.

Where do you install?

We serve Atlanta and communities across Northern Georgia within about 50 miles of Flowery Branch. Not sure if you are covered? Call or text us and we will let you know.
